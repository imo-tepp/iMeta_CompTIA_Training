# Understanding CyberSecurity Concepts

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the **AAA framework** and provide examples.
- Explain the **CIA triad** and it's importance.
- Discuss **password hygiene** and share good practices.

## Key Vocabulary

- *AAA* - ***Authentication, Authorization and Accounting***, a framework that manages user access and security.
- *Authentication* - Process of verifying identity of a user. E.g:
  - *username and password to prove the user identity*
- *Authorization* - what can be accessed by an authenticated user. E.g:
  - *You can read messages in your email after logging in.*
  - *You may not be able to change account settings without permission.*
  - *Authorization helps protect sensitive information by restricting access.*
- *Accounting* - Tracking of user activities and resource usage. E.g:
  - *Sending emails or changing settings, the actions are recorded*
- *CIA* - ***Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability***, a model for data security.
- *Confidentiality* - Sensitive information are kept secure from unauthorized individuals. E.g:
  - *Keeping account details private and secure*
- *Integrity* - Accuracy and reliability of data over time. E.g:
  - *Ensuring accurate balances and preventing unauthorized changes*
- *Availability* - Information is accessible whenever needed by authorized users. E.g:
  - *Access to the bank account is available at all times*
- *Password Hygiene* - Practice of maintaining strong and secure passwords.
  - *Use **complex passwords** that include letters, numbers, and symbols.*
  - *Change your **passwords regularly** to enhance security.*
  - *Never **share your passwords** with others.*
  - *Consequences of Poor Password Hygiene:*
  - *Implementing **two-factor authentication***
    - *Unauthorized access to accounts*
    - *Identity theft, where someone pretends to be you*
    - *Data breaches that expose sensitive information*
    - *Increased risk of financial loss*
