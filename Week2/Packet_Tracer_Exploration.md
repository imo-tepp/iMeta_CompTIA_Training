***Packet Tracer*** : is a network simulation tool that is used for designing and troubleshooting networks in a virtual environment.

    - Create various network topologies
    - Configure devices within the network
    - Simulate how networks behave under different conditions

### Types of Network Cables

***Twisted-pair cables*** : Used for telephone/data transmission, consist of pair of wires twisted together to reduce interference. They are widely used in (LANs). There are 2 main types of twisted-pair cable:

- **Shielded Twisted Pair (STP)** : It includes a shield to protect against interference, enhancing the overall signal quality.

  - Shielding reduces electromagnetic interference (EMI)
  - Twisted-pair cables are designed to cancel out noise
  - Improved signal quality leads to better data transmission rates.

- **Unshielded Twisted (UTP)** : More common and less expensive but offer less protection against external noise.

  - Connecting computer to switches and routers
  - Facilitating telephone communications
  - Supporting data transmission in Ethernet networks
  - Enabling internet access in homes and offices

***Coaxial Cables*** : Used for TV/internet connections, featuring a central conductor surrounded by insulation and a metal shield.

***Fiber-optic cables*** : Data is transmitted as light signal, offering high-speed and long-distance communication capabilities. There are 4 main components:

- **Core** : Central part of the optic fiber where light travels.
- **Cladding** : Outer part of the optic fiber that reflects light back to the core.
- **Buffer Coating** : A protective layer that shields the fiber from damage.
- **Jacket** : Outer layer that provides additional protection and durability.
